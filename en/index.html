About

What is Neck & Shoulder?

A 3-hour practical training specializing in a professional approach to the neck and shoulder area.

Add a new perspective to the skills you already have — that is Neck & Shoulder. The neck and shoulders are areas many clients care about. In this training you learn how to approach them with specialized knowledge and technique, so you can offer it as an add-on or as a main menu in your salon.

What You Learn

What you will take home

  • A specialized approach to the neck and shoulders
  • A new option to add to your existing menu
  • Confidence when explaining the treatment to clients
  • Technical grounding to support a higher price per visit
  • Technique built on a systematic body of knowledge

For Your Salon

Who this training is for

01

Facial salons

  • Add a menu that refines the look of the face line and neck area
  • Raise the value of your facials by working from the neck, shoulders and décolleté
  • Learn a treatment design that cares for the appearance of sagging

02

Relaxation salons

  • Add a neck- and shoulder-focused option to your current menu
  • Create a new main menu centered on the neck and shoulders
  • Stand out with a treatment where clients can easily feel a difference
  • Increase your average spend per client

03

Bust care salons

  • Strengthen your approach to the pectoralis major area, décolleté and the neck including the platysma
  • Design treatments that consider the bust line and face line together
  • Offer care that refines the look of the whole upper body

04

Upper-arm toning salons

  • Add an approach to the pectoralis major area, around the shoulder blades and the axillary (underarm) area
  • Offer treatments that consider the line of the whole upper body, not only the upper arms
  • Bring in a technique that adds easily to your existing upper-arm menu

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I take this training from overseas?

Yes. The training is held in person in Japan, and participants have traveled from overseas, including from Italy, to attend.

Is it held online?

No. Neck & Shoulder is an in-person, hands-on training held in Japan.

Where is the training held?

The location and venue vary by date. We will let you know after you apply.

How long is the training?

About 3 hours, in person.

How much does it cost?

General: JPY 110,000 (tax included). GMWBA members: JPY 88,000 (tax included). New GMWBA members can also attend at the member price.

Is a diploma issued?

Yes. A diploma is issued after you complete the training.

Can beginners attend?

The training covers both fundamentals and hands-on practice, but it is designed for adding the technique to a salon menu. It is recommended for people who currently run a salon or plan to open one.

Which salons can use this technique?

It can be used in facial salons, relaxation salons, bust care salons and upper-arm toning salons — as an add-on option or as a main menu.
